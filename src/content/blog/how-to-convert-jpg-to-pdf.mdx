Smartphones have made capturing receipts, whiteboards, handwritten notes, and identification documents instantaneous. However, sending someone ten separate .jpg files for an expense report or visa application is unprofessional and awkward to review.

Converting your photos into a single, organized PDF file creates a polished, standardized document that can be viewed and printed easily on any device.

## Why Convert JPG Images to PDF?

- **Universal Standard:** PDFs render with identical margins and sizing across iPhone, Android, Mac, and Windows.
- **Print Perfection:** A PDF standardizes image boundaries to standard page sizes like A4 or Letter, eliminating printer cropping issues.
- **Multi-Page Organization:** Grouping multiple receipts or portfolio photos into one continuous file prevents files from getting lost.
- **Smaller Combined Footprint:** Image streams are encapsulated efficiently without duplicate headers.

## Step-by-Step: Turning JPGs into a PDF

### Step 1: Select Your Pictures
Open the [JPG to PDF Tool](/tools/jpg-to-pdf). Click to browse or drag and drop your photos into the dropzone. You can upload multiple files at once.

### Step 2: Arrange the Sequence
Check that your images are listed in the logical reading order. If you also have PNG graphics or screenshots, you can use our companion [PNG to PDF Tool](/tools/png-to-pdf).

### Step 3: In-Browser PDF Generation
Click the convert button. Our client-side rendering engine calculates image aspect ratios, creates standardized A4 pages, centers the images with clean margins, and outputs a ready-to-use PDF document.

### Step 4: Download Your New File
Click **Download PDF** to save your file locally.
