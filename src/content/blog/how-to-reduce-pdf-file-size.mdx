Most enterprise email servers and public email providers enforce strict attachment caps:
- **Gmail & Google Workspace:** 25 MB limit
- **Microsoft Outlook & Office 365:** 20 MB limit
- **Yahoo Mail:** 25 MB limit

If your PDF exceeds these limits, your email will bounce immediately. Here are five practical methods to shrink your PDF file size so your messages deliver reliably every time.

## Method 1: In-Browser PDF Compression (Fastest & Most Effective)
The simplest way to shrink an oversized file is to run it through our dedicated [Compress PDF Tool](/tools/compress-pdf). It re-encodes internal data streams, strips unreferenced objects, and optimizes font dictionaries, often reducing file size by 40% to 70% without visible quality loss.

## Method 2: Delete Unneeded Pages
Do you really need to include the 10-page appendix or blank filler pages in your email attachment? Use our [Delete PDF Pages Tool](/tools/delete-pdf-pages) to trim unnecessary sheets and reduce weight instantly.

## Method 3: Clean Out Hidden Metadata
PDF documents accumulate hidden edit logs, XML tracking tags, and software stamps that add surprising amounts of overhead. Scrub these out with our [Remove PDF Metadata Tool](/tools/pdf-metadata-remover).

## Method 4: Split Into Multi-Part Attachments
If you must preserve every high-resolution chart in a lengthy report, split the document into two or three logical parts using our [Split PDF Tool](/tools/split-pdf) and send them in separate emails.

## Method 5: Optimize Embedded Images
When compiling scans or photos into a PDF, avoid raw uncompressed BMP or TIFF formats. Use our [JPG to PDF Tool](/tools/jpg-to-pdf) to ensure images are calibrated for standard page dimensions before compilation.
