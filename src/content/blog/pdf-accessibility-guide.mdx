Over 1 billion people worldwide live with disabilities, many of whom rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnifiers, and braille displays to read digital documents. When a PDF is poorly structured or consists solely of flattened images, screen readers cannot interpret the content, effectively locking users out.

Ensuring your PDFs are accessible is not only an ethical imperative — it is also a legal requirement under regulations such as the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)**, **Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act**, and the **European Accessibility Act (EAA)**.

## Core Pillars of an Accessible PDF

### 1. Real Digital Text (Not Scanned Images)
A screen reader cannot read pixels. Every word in the document must exist as true computer-readable text. If you have an image-based scan, convert it into an editable text document first using our [PDF to Word Tool](/tools/pdf-to-word).

### 2. Logical Heading Hierarchy
Just as sighted readers scan bold headings to understand document structure, screen-reader users navigate by heading levels. Maintain a strict order:
- One main document title (H1)
- Major section dividers (H2)
- Subsections and subsections within them (H3)

### 3. Clear Document Metadata
Screen readers announce the title and language of a document when opening it. Ensure your document title and author fields are accurately populated. You can check what information is currently stored in your file using our [View PDF Metadata Tool](/tools/pdf-metadata-viewer).

### 4. Color Contrast & Legible Typography
Ensure strong contrast between text and background colors (at least 4.5:1 ratio for regular text under WCAG 2.1 guidelines). Avoid light gray text on white backgrounds.

## Verifying Your Accessible Documents

Before distributing your document to the public:
1. Open the file in our [PDF Preview Tool](/tools/pdf-preview) and test text selection using your keyboard.
2. Verify that pages appear in logical reading sequence using our [Reorder PDF Pages Tool](/tools/organize-pdf).
3. If combining multiple accessible reports, ensure bookmarks and page tags remain intact by using our [Merge PDF Tool](/tools/merge-pdf).

By designing documents with accessibility in mind, you ensure your content reaches and benefits every reader.
