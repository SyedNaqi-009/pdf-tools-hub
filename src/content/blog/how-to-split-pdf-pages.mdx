Large PDF files often bundle together dozens of disparate sections: monthly bank statements grouped into a yearly binder, an entire textbook when you only need chapter 3, or a combined closing disclosure containing pages intended for different parties.

Splitting your PDF allows you to isolate exactly the pages you need and discard the rest. Here is how to split documents in your browser without uploading files to external servers.

## When Should You Split a PDF?

- **Distributing Relevant Sections:** Send team members only the specific pages of a report that pertain to their department.
- **Bypassing Upload Restrictions:** When portal limits prevent uploading a 100-page document, splitting it into smaller parts solves the problem.
- **Isolating Single Forms:** Separate a single signed contract page or receipt out of a bulky packet.
