The traditional workflow for signing documents was notoriously tedious: receive an email attachment, find a working printer, print the document, sign it with a pen, find a scanner, and email it back.

Today, electronic signatures are standard practice worldwide. Here is how you can sign any PDF document directly in your browser in under 60 seconds — completely free and 100% private.

## Are Electronic Signatures Legally Binding?

Yes. In the United States, the **ESIGN Act of 2000** and the **Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA)** give electronic signatures the same legal validity as traditional ink signatures for almost all commercial agreements, employment offers, and real estate leases.

In the European Union, the **eIDAS Regulation** establishes a comprehensive legal framework recognizing electronic signatures across all member states. Similar laws exist in Canada, the UK, Australia, and worldwide.
