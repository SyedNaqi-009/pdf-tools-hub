Few things are more frustrating than opening a scanned contract or mobile photo document, only to find the pages displayed sideways or completely upside down. Having to tilt your head or struggle with temporary viewer rotation controls slows down reading and looks unprofessional when sent to clients.

Our online tool allows you to permanently fix PDF page orientation in seconds right inside your browser.

## Why Do Scanned PDFs End Up Sideways?

- **Automatic Feeder Misalignments:** High-speed document scanners frequently feed pages in landscape orientation to maximize scanning speed.
- **Mobile Camera Orientation Flags:** When photographing documents with smartphones, the camera EXIF orientation metadata may not translate accurately into the PDF container.
- **Mixed Formats:** Documents that combine portrait text pages with landscape spreadsheets often have inconsistent page matrices.

## How to Permanently Rotate Your PDF

1. Open the [Rotate PDF Tool](/tools/rotate-pdf).
2. Select your document.
3. Choose the desired rotation angle:
   - **90° Clockwise:** Best for landscape scans that need to turn portrait.
   - **180° Flip:** Fixes completely upside-down documents.
   - **270° (90° Counter-Clockwise):** Turns pages to the left.
4. Click **Rotate PDF**. The rotation matrix is written permanently into the PDF dictionary, guaranteeing that every recipient sees the document correctly oriented.
