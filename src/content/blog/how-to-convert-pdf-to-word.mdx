## Digital PDFs vs Scanned Image PDFs

Before converting, it is important to know the difference between two types of PDFs:

### 1. Digital (Native) PDFs
These documents were created directly from applications like Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or InDesign. They contain real computer-readable text strings and font positioning data. Our [PDF to Word Tool](/tools/pdf-to-word) parses these files with high accuracy, preserving paragraphs, headings, and structure.

### 2. Scanned (Bitmap) PDFs
These are essentially photographs of paper pages wrapped inside a PDF container. Because they consist of pixels rather than text characters, they require Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to convert images into machine-readable text.

Once you finish editing your document in Word, you should convert it back to PDF before sending it to clients or colleagues. Why? Because Word files can display differently depending on the recipient computer fonts and software versions.
